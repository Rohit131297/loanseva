Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do you charge any upfront consultation fees?

No! We are an authorized Direct Selling Agent (DSA) partner with leading banks. Our advisory and assistance are 100% free of charge for borrowers.

2. What is the minimum CIBIL score required for approval?

A credit score of 700+ ensures faster approvals and higher loan limits. However, if your score is slightly lower, we also have tie-ups with NBFC partners that approve loans based on overall income.

3. How fast can my loan get disbursed?

Personal loans are disbursed within 24 to 48 hours. Home and Business loans generally take 3 to 5 business days after complete document submission.
